## Detailed Comparison

- **GitHub Actions** → 33% market share, 2000 min/mo free tier, ~4000 actions in the marketplace
- **GitLab CI/CD** → 20% share, all-in-one DevSecOps platform with Auto DevOps capabilities
- **Jenkins** → 25% share, 1800+ plugins, high legacy modernization demand
- **CircleCI** → ~12% share, strong caching and parallelism performance
- **Tekton** → Kubernetes-native, CNCF Graduated, growing adoption in K8s teams
- **Drone CI** → Open source, lightweight, 33k+ GitHub stars, Docker-based pipelines
